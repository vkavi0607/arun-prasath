Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the portfolio and advisory website of Arun Prasath P ("we", "us", "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us through this website. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and safeguard your details when you submit an inquiry through our contact form.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us. This includes:

  • Identity Details: Your name and company/organization affiliation.
  • Contact Information: Your email address and phone number (if communicating via WhatsApp).
  • Message Context: Any business challenges, requirements, or details you write inside the message block.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is handled strictly for business validation and correspondence. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Respond directly to your business inquiries, partnership ideas, or consultation requests.
  • Validate professional credentials before scheduling advisory calls.
  • Provide strategic recommendations or material handling audits as requested.

We do not rent, sell, or share your information with any third-party marketing companies.

4. Third-Party Integrations & Hosting

Our website utilizes several secure third-party components to handle operations:

  • Netlify: Hosts our static web assets under safe CDN caching guidelines.
  • Formspree: Manages contact form validation and secure transmission routing directly to our email mailbox.
  • WhatsApp (wa.me): Enables instant redirect links for direct encrypted mobile chat submissions.

5. Security Guidelines

We apply robust administrative and technical measures to protect your submissions. However, no data transmission over the Internet is guaranteed to be 100% secure. We encourage you to avoid submitting sensitive proprietary information or passwords inside the free-text message box.
